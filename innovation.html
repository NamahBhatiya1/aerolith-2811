Innovation and impact

What makes Aerolith different, and how it fits the UAE’s goals.

What is new here

The key innovation lies in combining quarry dust utilisation, acoustic agglomeration and mineral-based carbon capture into one integrated waste-management concept.

Dual environmental benefit

Addresses airborne quarry dust pollution while exploring CO₂ capture.

Waste-to-wealth

Transforms collected quarry dust into potentially useful construction bricks.

Advanced dust collection

Acoustic agglomeration improves the collection of fine dust particles.

Integrated technologies

Geopolymerisation, acoustic agglomeration and mechanochemical carbon capture work together in one concept.

Circular economy

Promotes resource recovery, waste reduction and sustainable construction practices.

Four technologies, one loop

Each technique is studied separately. Aerolith links them, so the dust one step collects feeds the next, and one waste stream is tackled twice: as bricks and as captured CO₂.

Built for the UAE’s goals

Aerolith is a student project, and the UAE’s national agenda is why it matters here.

Education 33

Dubai’s education strategy puts the learner at the centre and values skills such as critical thinking, creativity and problem-solving. Aerolith is a hands-on project of that kind: students pick a real problem, then design and test a solution.

Education

Circular economy

The UAE is promoting a circular economy, which keeps materials in use and cuts waste. Aerolith turns quarry waste into bricks instead of discarding it.

Waste

Net Zero 2050

The UAE has committed to net zero emissions by 2050. Aerolith explores one small contribution: locking CO₂ into stable carbonates and cutting dust. Its real carbon benefit is not yet measured.
