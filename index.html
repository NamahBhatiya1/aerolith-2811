Aerolith

Quarry dust, turned into bricks.

Quarries throw dust and CO₂ into the air. Our science project asks: what if we caught both, turned the dust into bricks and locked the CO₂ into stable carbonate?

One waste stream, two pathways

Aerolith starts from quarry waste and splits it into two pathways that share one goal. Dust collected in Pathway B can also be the raw material for Pathway A.
