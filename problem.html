The problem

Quarries put dust and CO₂ into the air. Here is why that matters.

Quarries make more than rock

Quarrying breaks up rock, and in the process it creates massive amounts of CO₂ and dust. Together they cause air pollution and environmental degradation around the site.

See our fix

  • Fine dust

    Once quarry dust is airborne it spreads and is hard to catch, so collecting it close to the source matters.

  • Carbon dioxide

    Quarrying releases large amounts of CO₂, so cleaner air means dealing with gas as well as dust.

  • Environmental degradation

    Left unmanaged, dust and emissions wear down the air and land around the quarry.
